\# Prompt Templates  

Generation Templates • Safety Templates • Engine Templates • Workspace Templates



Prompt Templates define the structure and rules for AI-generated content inside DevAiCoreX. Templates ensure consistent output across all engines and workspaces.

Prompt templates provide:



\- structured generation patterns

\- engine-specific templates

\- workspace-specific templates

\- build-specific templates

\- safety templates

\- lineage templates

\- memory templates



Templates define how content is generated.

Prompt Templates define structured generation patterns for DevAiCoreX AI agents. Templates ensure consistent, safe, and predictable output.
